About Us

About DV LLP

We help businesses keep their numbers in order and use them to decide what comes next.

DV LLP works with business owners, professionals and individuals on the work that keeps a business running smoothly: accounts, tax, funding, advisory and the systems behind them. Some clients come to us for one task, like a GST return or a loan application. Others want a partner who looks after their accounts month after month and helps them plan ahead.

Either way, our job is the same. Make the work clear, get it done properly, and leave you with a better view of your business than before.

What We Do

Services for every stage of your business

Management Consulting

Planning, budgets, cost control and MIS reports that show where the business stands.

Accounting and Bookkeeping

Day-to-day entries, reconciliations, payroll and year-end support.

Income Tax and GST

Returns, records, notices and planning within the law.

Project Finance and Advisory

Project reports, financial projections and funding readiness.

Working Capital and Loan Assistance

Preparing the documents lenders ask for and supporting you through the process.

Transaction Advisory

Reviews and support when buying, selling, merging or restructuring.

Custom Software and Work Automation

Tools that cut manual work and keep records and reports ready.

Who We Work With

Small and growing businesses, and the people who run them

Small and growing businesses, MSMEs, shop owners, traders, manufacturers, professionals and individuals. If your sales are growing but cash feels tight, your records are scattered, or too much depends on you personally, we can help.

How We Work

Four simple steps

  1. 1

    Understand

    We start with a conversation about your business, what’s working and what isn’t.

  2. 2

    Agree the scope

    We confirm which tasks we’ll handle, which documents we need and when you’ll hear from us.

  3. 3

    Do the work

    We take care of the agreed work and flag anything that needs your decision.

  4. 4

    Explain and follow up

    We walk you through the results in plain language and stay available for the next step.

What You Can Expect

How it feels to work with us

  • Plain explanations, without jargon.
  • A clear scope before any work begins.
  • Careful handling of your financial and personal records.
  • Advice your team can actually put into practice.
  • Finance, tax and technology support from one place.
