Transaction Advisory

Understand the numbers and risks before buying, selling or investing in a business. We help you evaluate the opportunity and prepare for the steps involved in the deal.

A proposed investment may look attractive at first, but the financial records, liabilities and working capital position need a closer look. We help business owners and investors review these matters before they agree on a price or transaction structure.

How we support your transaction

  • Due diligence: examine financial information, outstanding liabilities, working capital and relevant records to identify issues that need further review.
  • Valuation support: assess business or asset value using suitable methods and assumptions, with specialist valuation input where required.
  • Mergers and acquisitions: support planning, assessment of potential buyers or targets, negotiations and transaction coordination.
  • Deal structuring: assess payment terms, financing choices and tax implications, with legal input from the relevant professionals.
  • Financial modelling: estimate how the proposed transaction could affect profit, cash flow and borrowing under different assumptions.
  • Risk review: identify financial, commercial and compliance concerns, and highlight legal or regulatory questions for specialist review.
  • Private equity and joint ventures: assist investors and businesses with opportunity assessment, due diligence and transaction planning.
  • After the deal: help plan the handover of financial records, reporting and agreed business processes.

Make the decision with clearer information

We explain the findings, assumptions and unanswered questions in practical terms. This helps you decide what to investigate, which terms to negotiate and whether the transaction fits your business plans. The review can also cover a business sale, divestment or restructuring.
