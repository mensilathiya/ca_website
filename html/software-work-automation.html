Custom Software and Work Automation

Reduce repetitive work with tools built around the way your team works. We help you choose suitable software or build a custom tool for tasks your current system does not handle well.

If your team enters the same information in several files, prepares the same report every week or tracks approvals through scattered messages, there may be a simpler way. Tell us what the work involves. You do not need a technical specification to start the discussion.

Tools we can help you set up or build

  • Excel and spreadsheet automation: reduce repeated data entry, combine files, check records and prepare regular reports.
  • Billing and payment tracking: create invoice tools, track customer dues and set up payment reminders where the chosen system supports them.
  • Expense and approval tools: collect requests, route them to the right person and show what is pending or approved.
  • Stock and order tracking: organise inventory, purchases and order status in a tool suited to your workflow.
  • Business dashboards and MIS reports: bring agreed figures together so you can review sales, costs, collections and other business information.
  • Internal applications and portals: create forms and shared tools for tasks such as customer enquiries, job tracking or document collection.
  • Connections between systems: move agreed data between compatible tools, subject to available access and permissions.

Start with the task that takes too much time

We first understand the process and review sample data. Then we recommend an existing tool, an improvement to your current setup or a custom solution. Before development, we agree on features, cost, access permissions, data handling and support. We test the agreed workflow with your team and explain how to use it. Any ongoing maintenance or third-party charges are agreed separately.
