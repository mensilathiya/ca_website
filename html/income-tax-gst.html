Income Tax and GST Services

Get help with tax returns, GST records and department queries. We explain what applies to your situation, which documents are needed and what action to take.

Tax work becomes easier when your records are organised throughout the year. We support individuals and businesses with tax preparation, compliance and planning within the law. The work depends on your income, business activity and applicable requirements.

Income tax support

  • Prepare and file income tax returns for individuals, professionals and businesses, and assist with applicable forms.
  • Review personal and corporate tax matters, available deductions and tax implications before important business decisions.
  • Help organise information and responses for notices, scrutiny and assessments, with appeal support where applicable.
  • Advise on NRI and cross-border tax matters, including relevant tax treaty considerations.
  • Support transaction tax reviews and tax audit requirements, with work requiring statutory sign-off undertaken by appropriately qualified professionals.

GST support

  • Assist with GST registration and preparation and filing of applicable returns.
  • Match sales, purchase and accounting records with GST return data to identify differences.
  • Review input tax credit records, meaning GST paid on eligible business purchases that may be used against GST liability.
  • Assist with annual returns and reconciliation statements where applicable.
  • Support responses to GST notices, departmental audits, assessments and appeals within the agreed scope.

Know what needs your attention

We help you understand record gaps and filing requirements before they become harder to resolve. For a notice or pending matter, we first review the communication and supporting records, then explain the next steps.
