Project Finance and Advisory

Plan the money your project needs before you commit to it. We help you assess costs, prepare financial projections and put together information for funding discussions.

A new factory, an additional branch or an expansion needs more than an estimate of construction and equipment costs. You also need to understand expected income, running expenses, working capital and repayments. We bring these numbers together so you can assess whether the project is workable.

Support for new and existing projects

  • New projects, also called greenfield projects: assess the funding needed to set up a new business or facility.
  • Expansion and modernisation, also called brownfield projects: assess the cost and funding needs of upgrading or expanding an existing operation.
  • Feasibility studies: review project assumptions, expected demand, costs and commercial viability.
  • Project reports and financial projections: prepare estimates of sales, expenses, cash flow, funding needs and repayment capacity.
  • Funding structure: assess the mix of owner funds and borrowing, and compare suitable financing options.
  • Loan documentation: help prepare financial information and respond to lender queries during appraisal.
  • Collateral and project coordination: assist with information needed for security review and coordinate legal or technical inputs from relevant specialists.
  • Transaction and post-funding support: review financial arrangements and help track spending, cash requirements and agreed reporting after funding.

See the effect of changing assumptions

We can test how lower sales, higher costs or a delay could affect cash flow and repayments. This gives you a clearer basis for deciding the project size and borrowing requirement. Funding approval and terms remain subject to the lender's assessment.
