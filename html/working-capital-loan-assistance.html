Working Capital and Loan Assistance

Find a financing option that fits the purpose of your borrowing. We help you assess your requirement, prepare documents and understand the terms offered by lenders.

You may need funds to buy stock, manage the gap before customers pay, purchase a property or acquire a business. Each requirement calls for a different approach. We review the amount needed, available security and repayment capacity before helping you explore relevant options.

Business finance assistance

  • Cash credit and overdrafts: explore borrowing facilities for day-to-day business needs, including dropline and mortgage-backed overdraft options where suitable.
  • Bank guarantees and letters of credit: assist with documents for bank-backed commitments and supplier payment arrangements.
  • Export finance: assess packing credit, including foreign currency packing credit where available, to fund eligible export orders.
  • Bill and letter of credit discounting: explore finance against eligible bills or payment commitments.
  • Foreign exchange planning: help assess forward contract options for managing currency exposure in relevant business transactions.

Property and acquisition finance assistance

  • Home loans, loans against property and funding for commercial or industrial property purchases.
  • Lease rental discounting, which uses eligible future rental income as the basis for financing.
  • Finance for asset buy-outs and business acquisitions, based on the proposed transaction and lender requirements.

Understand the cost before you proceed

We help you review interest, fees, security requirements and repayment terms so you can assess the overall commitment. We provide advisory and application support; we do not lend money. Approval, the sanctioned amount and final terms are decided by the lender.
