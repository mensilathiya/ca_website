Our Team

The people behind DV LLP

DV LLP brings together chartered accountancy, company secretarial and technology skills in one team. That means your accounts, tax, compliance and the systems that run them can be handled together, by people who talk to each other.

Om Dayani

Chartered Accountant (CA)

Om looks after accounting, income tax, GST and financial advisory work. He helps clients keep their books in order, meet their tax requirements and prepare the numbers needed for funding and business decisions.

Paresh Vekaria

Company Secretary (CS)

Paresh handles company law and secretarial matters. He helps businesses with registrations, statutory filings, board and shareholder documentation, and staying on the right side of corporate regulations.

Mukesh Dayani

Company Secretary (CS)

Mukesh supports clients on corporate compliance, governance and documentation for restructuring, joint ventures and other business changes, so every step is properly recorded and filed.

Neel Vekaria

B.Tech, Technology and Automation

Neel leads custom software and work automation. He builds tools that reduce manual work, connect business records and make reports easier to produce, so the rest of the team and our clients spend less time on routine tasks.
